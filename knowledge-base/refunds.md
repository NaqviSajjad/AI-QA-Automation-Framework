# Refunds

## Refund window

Acme Cloud refunds any subscription payment in full if the request is made within 14 days of
the charge date. This applies to monthly and annual plans and to seat top-ups. Requests made
after 14 days are assessed case by case and are normally issued as account credit rather than
a payment reversal.

Annual plans cancelled after the 14-day window are refunded on a prorated basis for whole
unused months, minus any discount that was granted for committing to a full year.

## How a refund is issued

Refunds are always returned to the original payment method. Acme Cloud cannot send a refund
to a different card, a different bank account, or a third party. If the original card has
expired or been closed, the customer's bank routes the refund to the replacement account
automatically; if the account was closed entirely, the customer must contact their bank.

Processing time after approval:

- Credit and debit cards: 5 to 10 business days
- SEPA direct debit: 3 to 5 business days
- PayPal: 1 to 3 business days
- Account credit: immediate

## What is not refundable

One-off professional services, training days already delivered, and usage-based overage that
has already been consumed are not refundable. Taxes are refunded in proportion to the refunded
amount.

## Duplicate-charge refunds

A confirmed duplicate charge is not treated as a refund request and is not subject to the
14-day window. The duplicate amount is reversed as soon as the reconciliation job or a support
agent confirms it.

## What support can promise

An agent may confirm the refund policy, check whether a charge falls inside the 14-day window,
and raise the refund request. An agent must not state that a refund has been processed, give a
guaranteed arrival date, or quote an amount before the finance system has confirmed it.
