# Subscriptions

## Plans

Acme Cloud offers four plans: Free, Starter, Business and Enterprise. Free includes one user
and 1 GB of storage. Starter and Business are self-service and billed per seat per month, with
a discount for annual commitment. Enterprise is contracted and includes SSO, audit logs and a
named customer success manager.

## Trials

Every new workspace gets a 14-day trial of the Business plan with no card required. A trial
that is not converted drops to the Free plan automatically; nothing is deleted and no charge is
made. A trial can be extended once by 14 days by a support agent.

## Upgrading and downgrading

An upgrade takes effect immediately and is prorated to the day on the next invoice. A downgrade
takes effect at the end of the current billing period so the customer keeps what they paid for.
Downgrading below the number of seats currently in use is blocked until seats are removed.

## Auto-renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically on the anniversary of the start date. A renewal reminder is
emailed 7 days before an annual renewal. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the subscription
stays active until the end of the paid period, after which the workspace moves to the Free
plan. Cancelling does not delete data.

## Seats

Seats can be added at any time and are prorated to the day. Removed seats free up at the end of
the billing period. A deactivated user does not consume a seat from the moment of
deactivation.

## What support can and cannot do

Support can explain plan differences, change a plan, extend a trial once, add or remove seats,
and cancel auto-renewal. Support cannot apply a discount outside published pricing, backdate a
cancellation, or restore a plan for a period that has already been invoiced and paid.
